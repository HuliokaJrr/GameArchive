# 🎮 Game Archive — The Empire Update

**Build your store. Develop your studio. Dominate the gaming industry.**

Game Archive is an incremental management game where you start with a small video game store and gradually build a global gaming empire.

Buy investments, expand your business, develop original games, compete against rival companies, and unlock hidden secrets as your empire grows.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Incremental Gameplay:** Generate revenue, make sales, and continuously grow your business.
- **Business Management:** Purchase investments, hire staff, and expand your operations.
- **Game Development:** Build your own catalog of games and increase your influence in the industry.
- **Market Competition:** Compete against rival companies and use strategies to gain market share.
- **Research & Expansion:** Unlock new technologies, facilities, and business opportunities.
- **Prestige System:** Restart your journey with permanent bonuses and greater long-term progression.
- **Achievements & Secrets:** Discover milestones, hidden rewards, and secret codes.
- **Daily Missions:** Complete objectives to earn additional rewards.
- **Bilingual Interface:** Switch between Portuguese and English.
- **Local Save System:** Your progress is automatically saved in your browser.

## 🚀 Getting Started

No installation or external dependencies are required.

1. Clone this repository:

   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/game-archive.git
   ```

2. Open the project folder.
3. Run `index.html` in your browser.

You can also use the **Live Server** extension in Visual Studio Code for local development.

## 🎯 Gameplay Progression

Your journey begins with a small store and evolves through several stages:

1. **Startup:** Make sales and purchase your first investments.
2. **Growth:** Expand your business and build your first development team.
3. **Industry:** Release games, research technologies, and compete with established companies.
4. **Empire:** Dominate the market, unlock advanced expansions, and discover hidden content.
5. **Beyond:** Use the Prestige System to push your progression even further.

## 💾 Saving System

Game Archive uses browser LocalStorage to save progress automatically.

Your save is stored locally in the browser, meaning it does not automatically synchronize between different devices or browsers.
